Project 01

Sales Dashboard & Performance Analysis

Power BI dashboard tracking sales, KPIs, delivery performance, and YoY growth with DAX metrics and interactive views.

More Detail

Goal: Build a commercial performance dashboard to monitor revenue, orders, customers, shipping cost, delivery status, and customer satisfaction.

Tools: Power BI, DAX, Excel/CSV data preparation.

Implementation

  • Cleaned and standardized sales, orders, customers, products, payments, shipping, and review datasets.
  • Built a Power BI data model and configured relationships across key fact and dimension tables.
  • Created DAX measures such as Total Sales, Total Orders, Total Customers, AOV, Freight Ratio, YoY, and QoQ.
  • Designed dashboards by year/quarter/month, category, payment method, and delivery status.
  • Analyzed delivery time, freight cost, and customer rating relationships.
  • Added slicers and tooltips for flexible filtering by period, order status, shipping status, and product size group.

Outcome

Delivered an end-to-end KPI view, identified strong growth in early 2018, highlighted late-order risk linked with low review scores, and showed freight cost at around 14% of payment value to support logistics optimization.

Project 02

Customer Churn Prediction

ML model using Random Forest & Logistic Regression with SMOTE to predict churn risk and identify key drivers.

More Detail

Goal: Predict churn probability for bank customers and prioritize retention campaigns for high-risk groups.

Tools: Python, Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn, Scikit-learn, Imbalanced-learn.

Implementation

  • Prepared customer data with features such as credit score, geography, age, tenure, balance, product count, card status, and activity level.
  • Ran EDA to identify churn-related drivers, with notable effects from age, high balance, and low activity.
  • Built preprocessing steps including unnecessary-column removal, categorical encoding, and scaling.
  • Created train/test pipeline and trained Decision Tree, Random Forest, and Logistic Regression models.
  • Handled class imbalance using class weights, random oversampling, random undersampling, and SMOTE.
  • Evaluated with Accuracy, Precision, Recall, and F1-score; then tuned threshold to improve churn detection balance.

Outcome

  • Identified high-risk segments, especially older and inactive customers with high account balances.
  • Threshold optimization improved Precision-Recall trade-off for business retention use cases.
  • Final Logistic Regression baseline reached approximately F1-score = 0.31 after threshold tuning.
  • Recommended focused customer-care actions for high-probability churn segments to reduce attrition cost.

Project 03

E-commerce Fraud Detection

XGBoost model on 1.47M transactions to detect fraud in highly imbalanced data, simulating up to 84.4% loss reduction.

More Detail

Goal: Build a machine learning model to detect high-risk e-commerce transactions and support fraud investigation prioritization.

Tools: Python, Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib/Seaborn, Scikit-learn, XGBoost.

Implementation

  • Processed more than 1.47 million labeled transactions with significant class imbalance.
  • Performed EDA on amount, time, account age, and address behavior patterns.
  • Engineered features such as transaction amount log, account age, transaction hour, and billing/shipping mismatch.
  • Trained and compared Logistic Regression, Decision Tree, Random Forest, and XGBoost.
  • Applied imbalance handling techniques including class weight and random undersampling.
  • Evaluated model performance with Precision, Recall, F1-score, ROC-AUC, and PR-AUC.

Outcome

  • Selected XGBoost with undersampling as the final model.
  • Final model reached PR-AUC = 0.378 and Recall = 68.8%, aligned with high-fraud-catch priority.
  • Detected major fraud signals: high-value orders, new accounts, and midnight transactions (00:00-05:00).
  • Business simulation suggested up to 84.4% reduction in potential fraud loss versus no model usage.
